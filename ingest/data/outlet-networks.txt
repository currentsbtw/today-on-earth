# networks above do. Found by data on two windows (16 GKG files 2026-10-23 03:00-04:45 UTC and a
# 6 h state 2026-10-23 19:15 to 10-24 01:15 UTC): a domain whose reprint keys equal another
# same-country domain's in 80%+ of its records (2+ shared headlines) joins that domain's group.

# titles of a chain that the windows did not show are added by name and marked so.

# joined only by AP or dpa copy, aggregators (zazoom.it, inewsgr.com).
