# Surface brief: web/index.html (the map), with web/about.html and web/clip.html

Scope: the whole web app's visual world, replaced 2026-10-21. Mode: Operate (the map), Read (about.html), Experience (clip.html frames).
Audience and job: a phone visitor from a clip and a desktop explorer, as peers; see the planet glow, open a place, read what the world says about it, scrub the day, read the six signals.
Proof and content: live GDELT-derived data every 15 minutes; the broadcast; the methodology.
Constraints: map behaviour, data contract, keyboard access, phone sheet, clip safe areas, first paint must not get slower, no trackers (fonts self-hosted).
Chosen direction: Datamatics Field (seed 4ddf2d6c, challenger ikeda-datamatics, chosen by the owner over the assigned Departures Board on 2026-10-21 after four coded mockups). Memorable moment: the world's news as a pure black-and-white data field, the planet glowing white, numbers packed in the margins, one inversion per snapshot.
Unresolved: newsletter surface; whether the amber heat returns in any later theme (not now).

## Direction contract

THESIS: News coverage as a measured field in pure black and white; the map glows white on black and the margins carry the numbers. It refuses the glowing amber dashboard with card panels.

OWN-WORLD: #000 and #fff only for chrome, marks and type; greys only inside the map plate and the text hierarchy's muted steps. Azeret Mono at data density for every number, label and control; Source Sans 3 for the one large sentence and reading text; Silkscreen pixel blocks for the wordmark; a 16x16 one-bit pixel globe as the mark; an 8px dot grid in margins; hairline rules; a one-bit pixel observer mascot; no rounded corners, shadows, gradients or colour.

STORY: The visitor sees the planet lit white, reads one sentence of totals, understands brightness is coverage, opens a place and reads its numbers and headlines in a log; the day is a field of 96 bars they can scrub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: top line with pixel wordmark left, mono nav (MAP PLACES SIGNALS REPLAY METHOD) centre, UTC clock with seconds right; the sentence "N articles are pointing at M places." large across the top of the map; the map in a tick-edged frame filling the centre; left margin 200px a ranked mono places column, selected place pulled to white; right margin 280px the 96-bar coverage field (the replay), frame indices, a trend line and the recent-frames table; totals in mono along the foot; the observer mascot bottom right. Phone 390: wordmark line, the sentence, the map, then places and the bar field stacked; the sheet for a place.

FORM: Datamatics Field, dealt as a challenger (competitive verdict) against the assigned Departures Board; owner's choice; seed key 4ddf2d6c. Signature interaction: selecting a place pulls its column into white focus while the rest drop to faint bar patterns; a new snapshot inverts the whole frame for one frame (off under reduced motion). Motion grammar: steps, not eases; nothing fades.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
